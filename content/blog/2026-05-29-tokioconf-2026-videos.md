It has been a bit more than a month since the inaugural
[**TokioConf**][tokioconf]. As promised, here are the [video
recordings][videos]!

Again, I have to thank everyone who made the event possible, especially the
speakers. Putting together talks is a lot of work, and the talks were great. The
hallway track was also something to experience. We unfortunately cannot capture
that in videos. There were a lot of conversations and ideas that came out of it,
including a new idea for a [`spawn_compute`][spawn_compute] API in Tokio.

If you were not able to attend, know that we missed you. But there is also good news!

**We will be hosting TokioConf again next year!**

When: **April 26-27, 2027**<br/>
Where: **Portland, OR**<br/>

The website is still [**tokioconf.com**][tokioconf] though we haven't updated it
yet. You can sign up for email updates if you want to be notified when more info
comes out.
